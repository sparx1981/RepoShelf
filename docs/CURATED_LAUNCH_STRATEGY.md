Check recently validated demos, screenshots, licences and actual functionality before including a candidate; broad keyword matching alone is insufficient. ## Product-led launch

Positioning: **Try a working idea. Give your AI a head start.** Explain that the library helps people find reusable projects with demos and saved context, without claiming that every project is free to reuse or secure.

First audience: developers using Codex and Claude Desktop to prototype apps. The public demo experience is the acquisition path; saving favourites, submitting projects and connector setup are reasons to create an account. The current local MCP guide is useful but requires a clone and periodic pulls. Self-service, revocable member keys or OAuth are the next step before advertising a frictionless hosted connector. Never share the operator's API key as a member benefit.

Before announcing widely, choose ten excellent projects, verify their demos and previews, and record short clips showing the outcome: idea → live demo → compare requirements → build with a starting point. Build three shareable collections around specific jobs, such as a portfolio, an AI assistant and a small-business dashboard. Use existing direct listing links in posts; custom social preview images for listings and collections would improve sharing further.

Use a staggered launch: a small invited group first, then useful demonstrations in relevant developer communities, then a larger launch announcement if onboarding and retention work. A public guide with example prompts and candid connector requirements is more persuasive than generic launch copy. Invite repository creators to claim or submit listings, without implying paid promotions are public; promotions remain administrator-only test functionality.

Potential sharing loop: someone discovers a useful demo → saves it → shares its listing or collection → a new visitor tries it → signs in to keep useful finds. Creators can amplify their own listings if cards look good and attribution is clear. Share images, public collections and creator badges are suggestions, not features implemented in this change.

## Measure before expanding

Use the existing consent-based funnel: listing views, demo clicks, completed sign-ins, likes and verified forks. Add explicit connector-guide and setup-completion events only after defining what constitutes completion; opening the guide is not evidence that MCP connected. Exclude administrators and internal tests. Attribution from voluntary tagged campaign links can compare channels without collecting unnecessary personal data. Analytics consent means totals will be partial.

Judge progress by demo-to-save conversion, successful connector setup, repeat weekly use and shares that lead to useful visits. Do not promise virality or optimize only for page views. Resolve broken-demo reports promptly and add the dedicated support/privacy email before the public launch.

No external marketing messages or campaigns have been published by this work.
