Should Wheelhouse launch e-bike subscriptions in a second city next year?

For: founders and board
Horizon: next 12–24 months · Region: UK
Illustrative example — figures are placeholders

The short answer

Yes, if a pilot of 300 bikes reaches 70% fleet use within four months; otherwise wait a year and fix servicing costs first.
  1. KnownCommuter demand is concentrated within 8 km of the centre, where cycle lanes are continuous.
  2. EstimateServicing is the largest cost after the bikes themselves; margin depends on technician routes, not price.
  3. OpinionThe winner in each city is the first operator with same-day repairs, not the cheapest.
