# QA and discovery update — 4 October 2026

## Verified changes

- Hero rotates every eight seconds, wraps, offers pause/play and respects reduced motion. Rotation pauses during hero interaction, open dialogs, hidden pages and off-screen display.
- The editor exposes all thirteen built-in rows, including spotlight and editorial picks. They support editing, ordering and hiding without replacing category rows. Public unpublished rows remain hidden instead of reappearing through default fallback.
- Random ordering changes per page load; daily shuffle shares a UTC date seed. Both preserve relative order when filters change. Promotions sits beside Storefront editor.
- Demo/screenshot batches are eligible every two hours, with bounded work. Missing previews get priority by oldest attempt, failed screenshots retry after two hours, and healthy previews refresh weekly or after code changes. Two unavailable demo confirmations still require six hours between them.
- Analytics selected-date metrics consistently use UTC calendar days. Late responses cannot repopulate private analytics after sign-out. Server and client exclude administrator activity and honour privacy signals. Consent changes trigger the first eligible page view without creating identifiers before opt-in.
- Sync logs flag overdue successful runs and distinguish published discoveries from failed final preview publication. GitHub cron remains best effort.
